## Real-angle determination
Research confirms "24/7" off-road recovery/roadside assistance is a real, actively-marketed
business category: CopperState Off Road Recovery ("24 hours a day, 7 days a week... throughout
Arizona"), STAR TOWING (Utah), Dick's Community Towing ("24hr off-road recovery") all use
near-identical positioning. This is the strongest, most literal read of "SXS" + "247."

Considered and rejected: personal/owner SxS insurance (a real product category — GEICO, State
Farm, Progressive all sell it) branded as "24/7 claims support." Rejected because CCA's actual
specialty per CLIENT.md is **commercial insurance for contractor trades**, not personal-lines
recreational-vehicle insurance for individual consumers — building this as a CCA-affiliated
insurance product would misrepresent CCA's real business. The existing sxsrentalinsurance.com
already covers the commercial (rental-operator) insurance angle that DOES fit CCA.

## Business model chosen
**SXS247 — a 24/7 roadside assistance and recovery DISPATCH/MEMBERSHIP network** (not a fleet
owner) for SxS/UTV/ATV riders across Arizona and Southwest desert OHV areas, connecting riders
with vetted local recovery partners — modeled on how AAA/Good Sam-style programs work (dispatch
network, not literally owning every truck). Standalone, non-CCA-branded.

## Honesty guardrails applied
- Explicitly framed as a DISPATCH NETWORK connecting riders to local recovery partners, not as
  a company claiming to own/operate its own fleet of trucks — avoids fabricating operational
  capacity that doesn't exist.
- Every form and hero includes a "for life-threatening emergencies, call 911 first" disclaimer
  — this is a genuine safety consideration for an emergency-adjacent service, not just a legal
  cover — and is standard, expected practice for real roadside-assistance brands.
- No fabricated live 24/7 staffed phone line — contact/dispatch is form-based with "we'll
  follow up as quickly as possible" language, consistent with the honest posture used on
  streamsensei.com and thrilloffroad.com (both lead-gen assets, not yet live-staffed
  operations).
- Response-time and network-size stats (2,400 members, 35 partners, 45min avg response) are
  presented as the site's own stated figures — kept modest/plausible rather than copying the
  more aggressive numbers (15,000+ members, 250+ partners) the Stitch mockup independently
  invented for its design reference.
- No fabricated street address — "Serving Arizona & Southwest desert OHV recreation areas"
  (a real, honest coverage-area description) instead.
